Travis Gerke, ScD

Head of Applied Intelligence, PCCTC (an MSKCC subsidiary)

AI, data science, and biostatistics for clinical research

I lead Applied Intelligence at the Prostate Cancer Clinical Trials Consortium, where I own the strategy for AI and LLM adoption across a portfolio of oncology trials: agentic coding workflows inside governed cloud environments, cross-study data platforms, and LLM-assisted CDISC automation headed toward submission-ready datasets.

I trained as a biostatistician and epidemiologist, and I still keep my hands in the most demanding statistical work, from Bayesian adaptive trial designs to causal inference in real-world evidence. I co-authored Causal Inference in R (CRC Press), and over fifteen years I’ve built data science teams across academia, industry, and startups.
